Overview

A drone-based simulation focused on cinematic camera experience rather than competitive racing. The project emphasizes capturing high-quality footage using multiple in-game camera systems, supporting both exploration and structured scene recording within a controlled environment.

My Contribution
Vy Lo — Main Coder
Drone movement system — Rigidbody-based flight control with thrust, torque, drag, and stability tuning
Camera system — multiple cinematic modes (follow, orbit, freecam, cinematic lock)
Media capture — photo and video recording displayed on a large in-game screen UI
Interaction systems — crash vibration, battery drain, and return-to-charging loop
Partner: environment design & scene composition
Process
STEP 01
Flight & Movement System
Built Rigidbody-based drone movement with tuned thrust, torque, and drag for stable cinematic control. Iterated sensitivity curves for smooth framing at low speeds.
STEP 02
Camera Modes System
Implemented multiple cinematic camera modes — follow cam, orbit, freecam, and cinematic lock — switchable at runtime for flexible recording setups.
STEP 03
Photo & Video Capture
Built in-game capture system outputting photo and video to a large screen UI, simulating a real drone media workflow within the game world.
STEP 04
Battery & Interaction Loop
Added crash vibration feedback, progressive battery consumption, and a charging station return mechanic to create a complete gameplay loop.
Outcome
A cinematic drone simulation centered on camera-driven storytelling — enabling players to capture dynamic photos and videos through flexible camera systems while managing environmental interaction and energy constraints.
