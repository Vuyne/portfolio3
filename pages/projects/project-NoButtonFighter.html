No Button Fighter

Unity C# SolidWorks 3D Printing Serial Communication Leap Motion SDK
PROCESS 01
PROCESS 02
RESULT
Overview

A gesture-controlled 3D fighting game built in Unity where players fight entirely without buttons. Using an Ultraleap hand tracking sensor, real-time palm velocity and position data is mapped to in-game actions — punches, dodges, fireballs, and movement — making the body itself the controller. A custom 3D-printed mount and serial communication platform complete the physical experience.

Process
STEP 01
Concept & Hardware Design
Designed the physical stewardplatform for the Ultraleap sensor in SolidWorks, then 3D-printed and assembled the housing. Established serial communication protocol between the motion platform and Unity.
STEP 02
Gesture Mapping
Integrated the Leap Motion SDK to read real-time palm velocity and position. Mapped forward thrust to punches, lateral movement to walking, vertical lift to dodge, and dual-hand push to fireball.
STEP 03
AI Opponent & Combat System
Built an autonomous OpponentAI that navigates toward the player, executes randomized attack combos with cooldown logic, and responds to damage with hit animations and health reduction.
STEP 04
Polish & Game Loop
Added particle effects on each attack, spatial audio with randomized hit sounds, background music, win/lose panels, and a game manager handling scene transitions and time control.
Outcome
A fully playable fighting game controlled entirely through bare hands — no keyboard, no mouse, no gamepad. The system reliably distinguishes jabs, hooks, dodges, and fireballs in real time using palm velocity thresholds, demonstrating a novel physical-digital interaction model for gaming.
