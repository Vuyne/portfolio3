Overview

Physical build of an F1 simulation rig under Red Bull Racing mentorship. Work spans structural assembly, precision deck fabrication, and haptic feedback integration — routing ButtKicker tactile transducers through a custom-drilled platform designed for maximum vibration transfer to the driver.

My Contribution
Vy Lo — Simulation Engineer
Assembled and positioned the metal TV stand for the simulator display — structural base for the full rig layout
Drilled the simulation deck using a provided template, creating openings at specified locations for ButtKicker cable routing
Researched optimal ButtKicker mounting position — identified lever-effect placement on seat frame away from support points to maximise vibration amplitude
Drilled pilot holes with Dremel and installed heat-set threaded inserts via soldering iron, creating durable mounting points for all seat hardware

Process
WEEK 01
Structural Setup
Assembled metal TV stand and positioned it as the structural anchor of the simulator display. Drilled the deck using a provided template for ButtKicker cable routing.
WEEK 02
Haptic Research & Deck Fabrication
Researched ButtKicker placement: direct mount to rigid seat frame, lever-effect positioning away from support points, rubber isolation between seat and deck. Installed heat-set inserts for hardware mounting.
ONGOING
Component Installation
Routing amplifier and cables inside the deck, mounting ButtKicker to seat frame at the researched position. Preparing for full system integration.
Outcome
A precision-fabricated simulator deck with heat-set mounting points and a vibration system engineered for maximum haptic feedback — seat-mounted, lever-positioned, and isolated from the platform for driver-focused feel.
