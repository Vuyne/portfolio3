Overview

A multi-algorithm ML classification study on the Iris dataset — one of the most studied benchmarks in pattern recognition. Multiple classifiers are trained, evaluated, and compared side-by-side, with decision boundary visualisation to illuminate how each model partitions feature space.

Process
STEP 01
Data Loading & EDA
Loaded the Iris dataset via sklearn, generated pair plots and feature distribution charts to understand separability of the three species classes.
STEP 02
Model Training & Comparison
Trained KNN, SVM, Decision Tree, Logistic Regression, and Random Forest classifiers. Applied train/test splits and cross-validation for robust evaluation.
STEP 03
Decision Boundary Visualisation
Reduced features to 2D pairs and plotted decision boundaries for each classifier, revealing differing geometric strategies for class separation.
STEP 04
Evaluation & Reporting
Generated confusion matrices, classification reports, and accuracy scores. Documented findings with clear comparison tables and visual summaries.
Outcome
A structured benchmark demonstrating how different algorithm families approach the same classification problem — with clear visualisation of why some models generalise better than others on low-dimensional, linearly-separable data.
